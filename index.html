Habit Tracker, but minimal

Track habit growth, not streaks.

Make any repeating task a habit by adding it to the Habit Tracker section.

All you'll see is how many times you hit that "Complete" button over how many times the task was scheduled. No streak tracking.

Each habit is unique. Not every habit needs a 10-day streak or a 99% completion rate to show how much you've grown and won.

For some habits, just 50% completion is a major step forward.

View the percentage you stuck to the habit from the last 90, 60, or 30 days to admire your growth, no matter what the numbers actually are.
